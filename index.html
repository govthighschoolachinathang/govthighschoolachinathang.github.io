About the school

Government High School Achinathang is a government educational institution located in the picturesque village of Achinathang in the Union Territory of Ladakh. The school is committed to providing quality, inclusive and accessible education to children from the surrounding rural and remote communities.

The school provides education from Balvatika to Class X and follows the CBSE curriculum, with emphasis on academic learning, character building, scientific temperament, creativity, discipline and the overall development of students.

Situated in the unique high-altitude environment of Ladakh, the school strives to provide students with opportunities to learn both inside and outside the classroom. Academic activities are complemented by co-curricular activities, sports, environmental initiatives, clubs, community participation and experiential learning.

The school aims to nurture responsible, confident and socially conscious learners while respecting the rich cultural heritage and traditions of Ladakh. With the dedicated efforts of its teachers, students, parents and the community, Government High School Achinathang continues to work towards creating a supportive and enriching learning environment for every child.

Our Vision

To be a centre of excellence in inclusive, equitable and quality education, nurturing confident, responsible and compassionate lifelong learners. We aim to foster scientific temper, critical thinking, creativity, environmental awareness and respect for Ladakh’s rich cultural heritage, while equipping students with the knowledge, skills and values to contribute to the nation.
